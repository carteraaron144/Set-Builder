# M3b Caves Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** execute task by task with superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** You can draw a cave: click an entrance on a hillside, click on to lay a winding tunnel, and mark points as chambers. The terrain is cut open at the mouth and hollowed along the tunnel, with no seam between the cave and the ground around it. Caves save with the scene, clear scatter where they break the surface, can be picked into, and export to `.glb`.

**Architecture:**
- **Saved inputs:** caves join `features` in `scene.json`. The heightmap is never changed by them.
- **Density field:** the ground's solid density is `s(p) = smin(h(x,z) − y, c(p))`, a polynomial smooth-min with `k = cave.groundBlend` (1.5 m) that rounds a cave's rim where it breaks the surface, where `h` is the carved heightmap and `c` is the cave volume's signed distance (negative inside the cave). Solid where `s > 0`.
- **Two kinds of chunk:** a chunk whose square meets a cave footprint is an *SDF chunk*. It is meshed by marching tetrahedra over the density field on a 0.5 m grid. Every other chunk stays a heightmap grid, exactly as today.
- **Why it's seamless:** away from caves, `s = h − y` is linear in `y` on vertical edges and linear across each chunk face. So every marching-tetrahedra vertex on a shared face lies exactly on the heightmap's edge line between the same two samples. The footprint margin (3 m) keeps `c` positive, and more than the ground blend (1.5 m) from `h − y` at the surface, on every face an SDF chunk shares with a heightmap chunk.
- **Where it runs:** `Ground` profiles caves next to splines and ponds. It owns `density()`, `isSdfChunk()`, the cave exclusion, and the footprint rects that dirty chunks. The mesh, picking, scatter and export ask `Ground` for chunk geometry.

**Tech stack:** as in M3a. No new npm dependencies and no server changes.

**Spec:** the design doc, https://claude.ai/code/artifact/6c15080f-9693-489e-89ed-54e596c066b9:
- the Caves row of the tools table: "Draw a tunnel spline from an entrance on a slope; brush to widen or carve chambers";
- Pipeline step 1: carved SDF chunks, heightmap minus cave volumes, joined seamlessly;
- Milestones: M3.

## Global Constraints

- **Determinism:** the density field and every mesh are pure functions of (layers, features). There is no `Math.random`.
- **No caves means no change:** a scene without caves gives byte-identical chunk geometry, exclusion and exports to M3a.
- **Formats:**
  - `SCENE_FORMAT_VERSION = 4`, `GENERATOR_VERSION = 4`.
  - v1–v3 scenes load with `caves: []`.
  - Anything newer is rejected as `newer-version`.
- **Limits:** at most 20 caves, each with 2–100 points. Points lie inside the scene square, with `y` in [MIN_HEIGHT, MAX_HEIGHT].
- **Tuning lives in data:** `features.json` → `cave`.
- **Performance:** one SDF chunk meshes in ≤ 30 ms on a 256 m scene. While a cave point is dragged, SDF chunks re-mesh at most once per frame (manual check: stays smooth).

## Decisions (defaults; change any in review)

- **Model:**
  ```ts
  interface CavePoint { x: number; y: number; z: number; chamber: number } // chamber radius, 0 = none
  interface Cave { id: string; radius: number; points: CavePoint[] }       // tunnel radius
  interface Features { splines: Spline[]; ponds: Pond[]; caves: Cave[] }
  ```
- **Sizes:**

  | Setting | Range | Default |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Tunnel radius | 1–4 m | 2 m |
  | Chamber radius | 3–12 m | 6 m |

  "Brush to widen" is the tunnel-radius slider plus chamber points. A free-form carve brush is out of scope.
- **Curve:** a 3D centripetal Catmull-Rom through the points, sampled every 1 m.
- **Volume `c(p)`:**
  - **Tube:** distance to the sampled polyline − radius, clipped to a flat floor at `centre.y − 0.6·radius`.
  - **Chamber:** a sphere at each chamber point, clipped to a floor at `centre.y − 0.6·chamber`.
  - **Joins:** tube and chambers combine with a polynomial smooth-min, `k = 1 m`. The cave meets the terrain with a hard `min`, so the mouth stays a crisp low-poly edge.
- **Footprint:** the cave's AABB + 2 m, as a height-sample rect. An SDF chunk is one whose sample square `[c·64, c·64+64]²` meets any footprint.
- **SDF grid:**
  - 0.5 m in x, y and z, aligned to the heightmap lattice.
  - The y range runs from min(chunk heights, cave AABB bottom) − 1 m to max(chunk heights) + 1 m, snapped to 0.5 m.
  - Six tetrahedra per cube on one fixed split, so neighbouring SDF chunks agree on every shared face.
  - Vertices are welded per edge. Indices are `Uint32Array` when needed.
- **Colours:**
  - **Terrain vertices** (`h − y ≤ c`) blend the heightmap colours of the two lattice samples on their edge. The heightmap colour code becomes `terrainColorAt(i, j)`, so both chunk kinds share it.
  - **Cave vertices** use `cave.rockColor` `#5d5a57`. Up-facing ones (normal.y > 0.7, from the faces around them) use `cave.floorColor` `#4f6b3a` (moss).
- **Scatter:** a biome sample is cleared when `c(x, h, z) < cave.clearMargin` (1 m), that is, where the cave breaks the surface.
- **Picking:** `raycastTerrain` takes an optional density. With caves, a ray stops where `s` first goes positive, so you can click inside a mouth.
- **Cave tool (`C`):**
  - **Draw:** the first click on the ground sets the entrance at the hit point. Each later click adds a point at the hit's x,z with the previous point's y. Enter or double-click finishes; Esc cancels.
  - **Edit:** the selected point's height (y), chamber on/off with its radius, the tunnel radius and Delete are in the Feature panel. Points drag in x,z.
  - **Gizmo:** points and the centreline draw without a depth test, so they show through the ground.
- **Export:** an SDF chunk keeps its node name (`terrain_cX_Y`). Its `-col` mesh is the render mesh.
- **Out of scope** (later): pools in chambers (ponds still flood the heightmap only); stalagmite and crystal scatter; seeing into caves from the overhead camera beyond what the mouth shows; overhangs outside caves.
